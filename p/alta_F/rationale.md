## Intent
Make it the least amount of work for her to choose her look.
Sync her calendar once, and each day’s occasion becomes her outfit choice.

The look is ready before she opens the app: the night before and again in the morning, on her lock screen.

You see the best option from Alta, worn on her, but also the alternatives by:
• item
• whole set
• saved looks
• worn before

When the outfit is missing something, she sees it on her. That’s the moment to either add it to her closet or buy one.

## Golden workflow
1. Sync, once: Sync calendar → iOS permission → each day gets its occasion (she can change any day) → a look loads into each day.
2. The night before: a lock-screen notification: “Tomorrow · Client meeting 10:00”, with the look and the weather.
3. Open: the occasion is set from her calendar, and Alta’s pick is shown on her.
4. Alternatives: by item (tap a piece, swap from her own) · whole set (shuffle, three below) · saved looks · worn before.
5. What she wishes she had: the missing tan loafers are drawn dashed on her → “I have these” (camera, Alta fills the fields, the set is complete), or buy a pair, marked Ad.
