## Intent
Set the interviewer up for success, so an occasional interviewer can run the interview well. Each moment of the interview gets what it needs: before, they know what to ask and what they’re scoring. During, the questions go with them to the site, on paper or phone. After, the review comes first.

## Golden workflow
1. 1:50, Interviews: one row: Site visit, the two criteria, time, Jordan Ellis, Brighton substation.
2. Prep, on their key interview questions: three questions, each showing what it builds on from an earlier interview, plus what they score (with what each criterion means) and what Jordan asked.
3. Optional: show all criteria in place, or dismiss the guidance line.
4. Take it on site: print a carry card or send it to their phone, with a box per question.
5. After 3:00, the review leads: a rating and a recommendation, their 2 criteria scored first, then the rest → Submit scorecard.
6. Submitted: their initials join the panel.
