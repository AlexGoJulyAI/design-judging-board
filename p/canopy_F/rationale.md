## Intent
Set an occasional interviewer up to run the interview well, by giving each moment what it needs:
• Before: what to ask, and what they’re scoring.
• During: the questions go with them to the site, on paper or on their phone.
• After: the review comes first.

Each question shows what it builds on from an earlier interview, so the visit adds to the panel instead of repeating it.

## Golden workflow
1. 1:50, Interviews: one row: Site visit, the two criteria, the time, Jordan Ellis, Brighton substation.
2. Prep: three key questions, each showing what it builds on from an earlier interview, plus the criteria they score (with what each means) and what Jordan asked.
3. Optional: show all criteria in place, or dismiss the guidance line.
4. Take it on site: print a carry card or send it to their phone, with a box per question for notes.
5. After 3:00: the review leads: a rating and a recommendation, their two criteria scored first, then the rest → Submit scorecard.
6. Submitted: their initials join the panel.
