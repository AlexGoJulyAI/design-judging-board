Rationale

Order follows the clock. Before 2:00 the team’s three questions lead, each with the earlier interview it overlaps, in the plan’s words; his two criteria sit beside them with who already covered each. After 3:00 the review leads and prep folds to one line.

Fairness: the panel shows planned coverage and who submitted, never ratings or notes. Stages and criteria are told apart by position, labels and weight in neutral ink. Expected effect: more of Owen’s criteria scored with evidence, fewer repeats. Not yet measured.
