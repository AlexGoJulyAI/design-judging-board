## Intent
Move the outfit decision out of the morning rush, where users give up on Alta, and into the evening before. At 21:00 Alta reads tomorrow’s calendar and weather and asks her to pick between two looks, each labelled by what it’s for. In the morning the chosen look is waiting in a notification, so there is nothing left to decide. An evening plan also counts toward the north star directly.

• One question per screen, with at most two answers.
• Two looks rather than one: a choice keeps her in control without bringing back browsing.
• Adding clothes happens inside the flow: at most one gap question per evening, tied to tomorrow, answered with one photo.
• Shopping stays out of the core loop for now.
• Notifications show no event names (“Meeting, then dinner”), so her plans stay private on the lock screen.

## Golden workflow
1. Evening nudge, 21:00: “Tomorrow: meeting, then dinner. Pick your look.”
2. This or that: tomorrow’s moments (10:00 meeting, rain from 16:00, 19:30 dinner) and two looks built for them, such as Meeting first or Meeting to dinner → pick one, or Show two more.
3. One gap: “Rain from 16:00. Do you own a raincoat or trench?” → snap it, or keep the look as it is.
4. Set: “Set for Thursday.” She can still change her pick.
5. Morning, 07:30: a notification shows the outfit and “umbrella after 16:00”. She just gets dressed.
6. Plans changed: “Your 10:00 is now a video call.” → keep the look, or see two more relaxed ones.
7. No calendar: “What’s tomorrow?” → Office day, Meetings or Day off, and the two looks come from that plan.
