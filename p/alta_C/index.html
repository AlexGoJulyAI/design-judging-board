Tomorrow, decided tonight

The final flow, refined for a polished city wardrobe: espresso-black evenings, ivory and one champagne accent, a refined Garamond serif for headlines and one clean sans for everything else. More space, four type styles, and quick spring motion. Tap through the phone, or pick a step.

Palette

Quiet luxury

Espresso black and ivory, with champagne reserved for today, the chosen look and the streak. No sporty neon.

Type

Two faces, four styles

Cormorant Garamond for headlines: soft, high-contrast and quietly luxurious. Manrope for title, body and caption. Down from 17 sizes to 4.

Space

Room to breathe

An 8pt grid, 24px margins and 24px between blocks. Three time tiles became one quiet line.

Image

Styled like a boutique

Product photos as flat lays, with the name and purpose set beneath, as on a fashion site.
