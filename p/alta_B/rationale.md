## Intent
One tap from opening the app to a planned day. Today’s outfit is already shown on her, and “Wear this” records it as planned, which is what the north star (weekly retained outfit planners) counts.

• Sets come from her own closet.
• A set she has already worn twice isn’t offered on Home.
• Shopping appears only when a set is missing a piece, marked Ad.

## Golden workflow
1. Open: today’s set is already shown on her.
2. Swap one piece or the whole set, previewed on her.
3. Wear this: today fills in on the week strip.
4. Missing piece: it’s drawn dashed on her → shop for it, tried on her before buying.
5. Tap a day: past days show what she wore, with Wear again; future days show the plan. The month calendar opens from the icon in the header.
