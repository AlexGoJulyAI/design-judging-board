# B · “Today, evolved” (Alta mix, final3)

## Intent
One tap from opening the app to a planned day. “Wear this” is the planned outfit the weekly-retained-planners metric counts. Sets come from her closet; a set worn twice isn’t offered on Home. Shopping only appears for a missing piece, marked Ad.

## Golden workflow
1. Open: today’s set is already on her.
2. Swap a piece or the whole set, previewed on her.
3. Wear this; the week strip fills in.
4. A missing piece shows dashed → shop, tried on her first.
5. Tap a day: past days show what she wore (wear again), future days show the plan. The month calendar opens from the header icon.
