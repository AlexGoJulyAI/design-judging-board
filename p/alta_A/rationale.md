# Alta: Home redesign rationale

**North star:** weekly retained outfit planners (users who return and *select or plan* an outfit).
**Friction tackled:** too many steps to decide. Alex abandons Alta on Day 3 because finding a look takes longer than getting dressed.

## Product judgment
Home now answers "what should I wear today?" first. The old hero (friends' logged outfits) answered a different question, and suggestions sat below the fold. Recommendations now lead, and the social feed stays lower down to keep Alta's community feel.

## UX and interaction clarity
- **Today's picks** explains itself ("For your *client meeting · 10:00*"). The occasion is a tappable tag, and Ask Alta covers anything else.
- **Select:** tap a look, swap pieces, then *Worn today*. Two taps, with mix-and-match in a detail sheet rather than buttons on the carousel.
- **Plan:** *This week* shows planned outfits at a glance, and *Plan an outfit* opens a day picker. Both halves of the north star are one tap from Home.
- **Wear again** surfaces proven looks, fixing the retrieval problem from user research.

## Visual craft
Built from Alta's own imagery and type in an accurate iPhone 15 Pro frame: one primary action per view, a five-step type scale, 44px touch targets, no emojis.

## Business thinking
Shopping appears only inside the outfit sheet as a labelled, dismissible "Complete the look" suggestion. It is relevant at the moment of intent and never competes with the daily decision, so it stays nonintrusive while still creating partner revenue.

**Measure:** weekly retained planners; time to first selection; planned days per user; sponsored dismiss rate (guardrail).
