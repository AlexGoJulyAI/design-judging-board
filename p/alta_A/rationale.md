## Intent
Home answers “What should I wear today?” first. Before, friends’ logged outfits filled the top of the screen and the suggestions sat below the fold. Now the recommendation leads, and the social feed moves lower so Alta keeps its community feel without getting in the way.

Both actions the north star counts, selecting and planning an outfit, are one tap from Home:
• Today’s picks, each with the reason it fits (“For your client meeting · 10:00”)
• This week, with planned outfits at a glance
• Wear again, with looks she has already worn and liked

Shopping appears only inside the outfit sheet, as a labelled “Complete the look” suggestion she can dismiss.

## Golden workflow
1. Open: Today’s picks show looks for her day. The occasion is a tag she can tap to change, and Ask Alta covers anything else.
2. Select: tap a look → swap pieces in the detail sheet → Worn today. Two taps.
3. Plan: This week shows what’s planned, and Plan an outfit opens a day picker.
4. Reuse: Wear again brings back proven looks, so she doesn’t have to dig through her history.
5. Shop (optional): in the outfit sheet, a labelled “Complete the look” piece she can dismiss.
