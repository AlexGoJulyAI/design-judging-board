## Intent
Lead with purpose. Ten minutes before the site visit, Owen sees which interview this is and his two criteria, not contact details, a job description and a blank scorecard.

• The top bar shows the interview, time, place and candidate. The first card, “Your part · Interview 5 of 5”, lists Owen’s two criteria in the team’s words. The queue framing (“1 of 10”, “Submit and next”) is gone.
• Two panels, one job each: context on the left, the interview in time order on the right.
• The panel map shows who interviewed, what each covered and asked, and “Scorecard in”. It never shows ratings, recommendations or notes.
• When a question repeats earlier ground, the earlier question is shown as a plain fact.
• First-timers get six tips they can dismiss; Customize lets experienced interviewers hide sections or change the layout.
• Nothing is generated: all text comes from the team, the candidate or fixed Canopy copy.

## Golden workflow
1. Open: the top bar and the “Your part” card say what this interview is and what Owen scores.
2. Context, left: Overview (Jordan’s key points, tips, the panel so far, planned coverage), with Job and Rubric tabs.
3. Questions, right: the team’s questions, with any overlap with earlier interviews shown.
4. During: a note field for each criterion.
5. Score and submit: the scorecard follows the notes, with the due time beside Submit to keep it within 24 hours.
