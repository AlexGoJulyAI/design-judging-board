# Canopy interview page: how we tackled it

**Problem:** 10 minutes before his site visit, Owen sees contact details, a job description and a blank scorecard, with no sign of what to cover or what was already asked.

**Product judgment.** The page leads with purpose:
- The top bar shows the interview, time, place and candidate.
- The first card, "Your part · Interview 5 of 5", lists his 2 criteria in the team's words.
- The queue framing ("1 of 10", "Submit and next") is gone.

**Information architecture.** Two panels, one job each:
- **Left: context**, as tabs: Overview, Job, Rubric. Overview holds Jordan's key points, tips, the panel so far and planned coverage.
- **Right: the interview, in time order**: team questions, notes for each criterion, then the scorecard and Submit.

**Interaction and content.**
- First-timers get six dismissible tips.
- When a question repeats earlier ground, the earlier question is shown as a plain fact.
- Customize lets experienced interviewers hide sections or change layout.
- Nothing is generated. All text comes from the team, the candidate or fixed Canopy copy.

**Fairness and trust.** The panel map shows who interviewed, what each covered and asked, and "Scorecard in". It never shows ratings, recommendations or notes, and coverage is planned, not scored.

**Visual craft.** Canopy's warm-neutral style, one accent, AA contrast, keyboard access, resizable panels; responsive from 1440px to phone.

**North-star effect:** Owen knows his criteria in advance and has a note field for each, so every criterion gets scored with written evidence. The due time next to Submit keeps it within 24 hours.
