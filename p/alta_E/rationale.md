## Intent
Make choosing an outfit a few actions, and building the wardrobe nearly effortless. Alta’s value is making the decision easier, not generating more options, so Home leads with one outfit and a Wear this today button, both visible without scrolling.

• One outfit first, two alternatives behind it: several equal options bring back the decision fatigue Alta should remove.
• Weather and occasion are built in (Work, Meetings, Home, Out, Event), so there’s no blank prompt to fill in every day.
• The outfit is shown on a model, with the pieces alongside, so she sees how they work together and which one to swap.
• Morning shows today’s outfit; evening shows tomorrow’s. Wear again brings back past outfits.
• Partner products sit below the outfit, labelled, so shopping doesn’t compete with the decision.
• Add items adds confident items automatically and asks only about uncertain ones, with specific questions (“Navy or black?”) in a compact grid.

## Golden workflow
1. Set up: upload photos → Alta recognises and sorts the clothes → she answers questions only for uncertain items → she sees how many new outfits the upload unlocked → her first recommendation.
2. Open: one outfit for today’s weather and occasion, shown on a model.
3. Adjust (optional): swap a piece, or see two alternatives.
4. Wear this today: one tap records the choice.
5. Evening: tomorrow’s outfit leads, ready to plan.
6. Reuse: Wear again picks a past outfit without building a new one.
7. Shop (optional): labelled partner pieces below the outfit.
