## Intent
Turn the workspace from three panels (contact details, a job description and a blank scorecard) into one flow in four steps: Prepare, Interview, Score, Done. Owen’s two criteria, power systems knowledge and working with field crews, carry through every step. The brief reads in about two minutes, under the five-minute limit.

• Earlier interviews show who asked, which interview and what topics, never ratings.
• During the call, the screen shows one question at a time with a private three-word note, so Jordan, standing beside the screen, sees only the question.
• The scorecard stays hidden until the visit ends, so judging doesn’t start mid-conversation.
• The team’s third question is close to Lena’s earlier scenario. The plan can’t be edited, so the design flags the overlap.
• Built for a substation: saved offline on first open, can be sent to a phone, and light high-contrast type that reads in sunlight.
• Blind until submit; criteria and questions in the team’s words; nothing generated; every scorecard field kept.

## Golden workflow
1. Prepare, 1:50: “Your hour with Jordan covers two things.” Site visit, 2:00–3:00, last of 5. The two criteria, each with the team’s questions and one tip under each; the topics the four earlier interviews covered; the one fact about Jordan to go deeper on, and Jordan’s own question.
2. Interview: one question at a time, with a three-word note per question.
3. Score: ending the interview opens scoring, one criterion per screen, with the notes beside each.
4. Done: submit, and the panel’s feedback unlocks.
