Rationale

One page, organised around what Owen scores

Owen opens Canopy at 1:50, maybe from his truck at the substation, with ten minutes and no training. He needs three answers: what do I score, what do I ask, what should I skip. The page gives them in that order and nothing else.

The header says when, where and who, plus one line on the job. One strip lists what earlier interviews covered, who has submitted, and that their scores stay hidden until he submits. Below are two cards, one per criterion he owns. Each holds the team's definition, the team's questions and a notes box asking what Jordan said or did. A flag marks the question Lena already asked in similar form.

There are no tabs and no modes. Notes save as he types. When he presses Score, a 1–4 control opens under his notes in each card, and rating and recommendation open below. Every scorecard field is kept. A sticky bar shows the one next action and a ring of what's done.

Nothing is generated. Every line comes from the team, the plan or Jordan.

Assumptions to confirm with the Canopy team
  • The plan can record which criterion each question targets and flag similar questions. The hiring team sets both.
  • The "already covered" topics are short labels from each interview's "What it asked" entry.
  • The scorecard's Notes field is filled from Owen's notes. No field is added.
