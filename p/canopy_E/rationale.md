## Intent
One page shaped like the job: two cards, one per criterion Owen scores. Today’s page is built around the record (a profile and a form); this one is built around the interviewer’s hour. It cuts the workflow from 12 steps to 7, and the reading before 2:00 to about 150 words.

• One column, read top to bottom, with no tabs or modes: Owen interviews about once a month and won’t remember the tool.
• An “Already covered” strip shows the topics earlier interviews asked and who asked them, never ratings or notes.
• One flag: “Lena asked something similar this morning.” The plan can’t be edited, so the overlap is pointed out instead.
• Notes are typed under each criterion during the call, because notes not taken then are gone by scoring time.
• Scoring opens only on “Done interviewing · Score”, keeping judgment out of the conversation.
• A sticky bar shows the next action and what’s still missing, to keep submission within 24 hours.
• Tips collapse to a link; large type and tap targets suit a laptop on a tailgate.
• Facts carry their source, and nothing is generated.

## Golden workflow
1. Open, 1:50: Interviews → Jordan Ellis → the workspace for this interview, not a queue.
2. Orient: site visit, 5 of 5, 2:00–3:00, Brighton substation, and Owen’s two criteria with what each means.
3. Panel: Theo, Ravi, Lena and Nina are done, with the topics each covered, not their conclusions.
4. Candidate: the 12 MW solar upgrade, the Colorado PE license, and Jordan’s question about field time.
5. Plan: the team’s three site-visit questions, each tied to a criterion. Steps 1–5 fit in five minutes.
6. During, 2:00–3:00: notes typed under the two criteria.
7. Score: Done interviewing · Score → rate each criterion 1–4 with the notes alongside → overall rating and recommendation → Submit.
