## Intent
One screen for the site visit, before and during the interview. The interviewer asks the team’s questions, writes what Jordan said under each focus area, and scores without leaving the page.

## Golden workflow
1. Prep (Site visit · 5 of 5): the two focus areas lead, each showing who else probed it, with the team’s questions under it. Jordan’s card and the interview loop sit alongside.
2. How-to, if needed.
3. Start interview → the same screen in a during state: a timer, “Back to prep”, 1–4 score chips per focus area, questions checked off, a “What Jordan said” note under each.
4. Finish: the other criteria → Recommendation → Submit scorecard.
