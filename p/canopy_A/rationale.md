## Intent
One screen for the site visit, used before and during the interview. Owen asks the team’s questions, writes what Jordan said under each focus area, and scores without leaving the page.

• The two focus areas Owen scores lead the page, each with the team’s questions under it and who else on the panel probed it.
• Jordan’s card and the interview loop sit alongside, for reference.
• Starting the interview switches the same screen into a during state, so nothing has to be found again mid-conversation.

## Golden workflow
1. Prep (Site visit · 5 of 5): the two focus areas lead, each showing who else probed it, with the team’s questions under it. Jordan’s card and the interview loop sit alongside.
2. How-to: guidance on running the interview, opened only if needed.
3. Start interview → the during state: a timer, Back to prep, 1–4 score chips per focus area, questions checked off as they’re asked, and a “What Jordan said” note under each.
4. Finish: score the other criteria → Recommendation → Submit scorecard.
