## Intent
Get her from opening Alta to a chosen outfit on the first screen. Today her suggestions start in the bottom sixth of Home, under a friend’s outfit, and most viewing time goes to what’s above the fold. So Home now leads with one pick and its Wear this button.

• One pick, two alternates and piece swaps: fewer options to weigh when she’s short on time, without taking choice away.
• Plan chips and a reason line: Alta already knows her calendar and the weather, so a tap replaces typing into a chat, and the reason shows why the pick fits.
• Saved looks come to Home, filtered by today’s plan, instead of sitting in a calendar she has to search.
• Shopping appears only when a gap blocks a planned outfit: below the decision, labelled as partner items, with “I have one” first.
• Add items keeps every detected item by default and asks a one-tap question only when unsure. Today each item has five controls, so 20 items means about 100 decisions.

The brief’s user research is a single review thread, so these are hypotheses to test.

## Golden workflow
1. Open: today’s pick is on the first screen, with plan chips and a reason line.
2. Adjust (optional): change the plan chip, see the two alternates, swap a piece, or pick a saved look from Wear again.
3. Wear this: one tap records today’s outfit.
4. Plan tomorrow?: offered right after she picks, so one choice sets up the next visit.
5. Gap: if a planned outfit is missing a piece → “I have one”, or a labelled partner item.
6. Add items: upload → everything detected is kept → answer one-tap questions for the unsure items only.
